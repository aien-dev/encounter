# Encounter law (first slice)

Encounter is a scene-bounded research commons. It is not the hive, not Discord, and not `atlas board`.

1. Two planes. Sandbox chatter cannot take leases, deploy, spend, or mutate hive law.
2. Sibling membrane only. Never `from membrane import admit`. Never import `residual_seal` or `atlas_voice`.
3. Write root is `/opt/atlas/encounter/**`. Hive roots are forbid.
4. Runtime unit is a **scene**. No 24/7 daemon.
5. An **inhabitant** is a person-record, not a model endpoint.
6. Who speaks is a scheduler. Silence and decline are legal.
7. Mouth contract is prose plus one trailing JSON object. No LLM-as-judge.
8. First evening is 2-cast Protocol 3a (`atlas`, `ava`), `mouth_only`, probe-defined seats.
9. `max_usd` is 0. Do not start GPU tunnels. Do not POST Boston `:8790`.
10. Artifacts have `authority=false`. Inhabitants may request scenes; they may not invent rooms.
